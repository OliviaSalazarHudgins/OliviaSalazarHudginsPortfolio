I'm a creative professional specializing in social media management, writing, content creation, and creative direction, with a particular focus on fashion. As the sole in-house lead on PR, influencer relations, and creative strategy for The Anteros, a luxury womenswear brand, I manage the full scope of how a brand shows up publicly — from content calendars to press outreach to campaign direction.


My work spans organizing and directing large-scale fashion events, including a sold-out 400-person fashion show, to co-founding and running Sto Mercerie, a vintage designer handbag resale brand, where I handle everything from sourcing to marketing. Across my own platforms, the same approach has generated over a million organic views, which tells me the strategy translates — it's not just brand-specific instinct; it's a repeatable understanding of what makes content resonate.


What sets my approach apart is the lens I bring to it. My major at NYU Gallatin School was an individually designed concentration in Media, Culture, and Critical Theory with an emphasis in fashion and creative nonfiction — so I don't just create content; I understand the cultural and historical context that makes it meaningful. I truly love absorbing and creating media that resonates and builds meaningful impact within a community or brand.


In my free time, I write about how culture, history, and societal trends show up in fashion on my Substack, which keeps that critical perspective sharp and current.
